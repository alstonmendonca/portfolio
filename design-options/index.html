Dark, but done properly — a blue-slate ground rather than black, body text at full contrast (not the washed-out grey most dark sites use), warm amber as the single accent. Rounded cards, pill buttons and tags. The friendliest and most modern-product of the three.

Grid

Swiss and structural. Near-white, tight condensed headings, everything sitting on a visible ruled grid, one signal red. Denser than the others — more content per screen, sharper edges, no rounding anywhere. Closest in spirit to your current brutalist page, but properly typeset.
